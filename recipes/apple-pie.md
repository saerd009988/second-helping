steps:
  - "Pulse the flour, 1 tablespoon sugar and 1 teaspoon salt in a food processor. Add the frozen butter and pulse 8 to 10 times, until the largest pieces are the size of peas. Those visible butter pieces steam apart into flaky layers; butter worked to cornmeal gives you a crumbly, cookie-like crust."
  - "Drizzle in the ice water and pulse 5 or 6 more times, just until the dough holds together when squeezed in your fist. Divide into two disks, one slightly larger, wrap tightly and refrigerate at least 1 hour so the flour hydrates and the gluten relaxes."
  - "Toss the sliced apples with the 3/4 cup sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon, nutmeg and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Let them sit 45 minutes, tossing twice. They will shed about 3/4 cup of liquid — this is the step that prevents the hollow dome you get when raw apples collapse under a baked lid."
  - "Drain the apples over a bowl. Simmer the collected juice in a small saucepan over medium-high for 4 to 5 minutes, until reduced to about 1/3 cup and syrupy. Whisk the cornstarch into the cooled syrup, then toss it back through the apples."
  - "Roll the larger disk into a 13-inch round on a floured counter, working from the center outward and rotating a quarter turn every few passes. Drape it into a 9-inch pie plate and settle it into the corners without stretching — stretched dough shrinks back down the sides as it bakes."
  - "Pack the apples in tightly, mounding them slightly and pressing down to eliminate air gaps. Roll the second disk to 11 inches, lay it over the top, trim to a 1-inch overhang, tuck the edge under the bottom crust and crimp firmly. Cut four 2-inch vents through the center."
  - "Freeze the assembled pie 30 minutes. Meanwhile heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the lower third and a sheet pan on it. A cold pie hitting a hot pan sets the crust before the butter can leak out."
  - "Brush the top with egg wash and sprinkle with turbinado sugar. Bake on the preheated sheet pan at 425°F for 25 minutes, until the crust is set and pale gold."
  - "Drop the oven to 375°F and bake 40 to 50 minutes more, until the crust is deep brown all over — pale crust means raw-tasting flour — and juices bubble thickly and slowly through the vents. Tent with foil if the edges darken too fast."
  - "Cool at least 4 hours on a rack before slicing. Cutting a warm pie releases filling that has not set, and the slice slumps onto the plate."

## Tips

**Two apples, two jobs.** Granny Smith holds its shape and brings acid; Honeycrisp breaks down slightly and sweetens. All of one or the other gives you either applesauce or crunchy wedges in syrup.

**Watch for thick, slow bubbles.** Thin fast bubbles at the vents mean the cornstarch has not come to temperature yet and the filling will run.

**Cold dough, hot oven, preheated sheet pan.** Every one of those fights a soggy bottom.

## Variations

- **Salted caramel:** Drizzle 1/2 cup of thick caramel over the apples before the top crust and add flaky salt with the turbinado.
- **Cheddar crust:** Work 3/4 cup of grated sharp cheddar into the flour with the butter.
- **Lattice top:** Cut the second disk into ten 1-inch strips and weave them; skip the vents since the gaps do that work.
